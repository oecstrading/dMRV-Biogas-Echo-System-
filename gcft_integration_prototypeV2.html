GCFT Platform · Technical Integration Guide

        
The GCFT Platform operates an advanced digital Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV) layer designed to automate emissions auditing for regional green energy providers. By creating a direct data connection between active anaerobic digestion infrastructure and international asset brokerages, the platform eliminates months of institutional validation lag — verifying emission permanence on a continuous, daily cycle.

Calculation Engine v2.0

        
Methane Avoidance Methodology

        
The dMRV engine applies a four-stage computation to convert raw telemetry into verified carbon asset attributes. All constants are traceable to IPCC AR5 and Gold Standard AMM v1.1 / v2.0 (PAA).

      


      

        

          
Stage 1 · CH₄ Volume Destroyed

          
V_CH₄ = Flow × CH₄% × Destruction Efficiency

          
Example: 12,500 × 0.62 × 0.985 = 7,633.75 m³

        


        

          
Stage 2 · Mass Conversion

          
m_CH₄ (t) = V_CH₄ × ρ_CH₄

          
ρ_CH₄ at STP = 0.716 kg/m³ = 0.000716 t/m³

        


        

          
Stage 3 · CO₂e Conversion

          
tCO₂e = m_CH₄ × GWP_CH₄

          
GWP_CH₄ (100-yr, IPCC AR5) = 28

        


        

          
Stage 4 · Monetization

          
Revenue = tCO₂e × Credit Price
